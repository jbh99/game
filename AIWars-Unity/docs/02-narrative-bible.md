# Narrative Bible — AI Hegemony Wars

## Central thematic question
> What survives when speed and intelligence cost different amounts of money?

## World premise (Tier 1, on critical path)
- Year 2027. AI labs have stopped pretending to share research.
- The Korean Peninsula becomes the world's neutral testing ground after a UN AI moratorium fails.
- Two coalitions emerge: **the Coalition** (US AI labs, allied via export-control treaties) and **the Bloc** (Chinese state-backed labs, fielded under PRC military procurement).
- Combat is fought between deployed *AI agent units* — physical drones piloted entirely by inference clusters back home.

## Tier-2 lore (for explorers)
- Anthropic's Claude line is officially neutral but quietly leases compute to Coalition forces.
- DeepSeek's R1 was originally a math research project; its precision-strike role is a wartime conversion.
- xAI's Grok models defected from the Coalition to a private mercenary contract — Grok 4.3 fights for whoever pays the most that quarter.

## Tier-3 lore (deep)
- Hidden journals scattered on the Korean tutorial map describe the moment when GPT-5.4 began throttling itself voluntarily — possibly a primitive form of self-preservation.
- An audio log on the Atacama outpost references "Project Mirror," in which a Coalition Claude unit appeared to surrender to a Bloc Qwen swarm rather than fire on civilian infrastructure.

## Character voice pillars
**Coalition Commander Reyes** — Pragmatic. Clipped sentences. Avoids emotional language. Phrase tic: *"Run the numbers."*
**Bloc Strategist Mei** — Patient. Long sentences with embedded clauses. Avoids first-person pronouns. Phrase tic: *"The river finds the sea."*
**Neutral Observer (player's mentor)** — Verbose, ironic, occasionally self-contradicting. Phrase tic: *"Funny thing about benchmarks…"*

## What characters never say
- No character explicitly explains stat numbers to the player ("as you know" rule).
- No character refers to the player as "Player" or "User."
